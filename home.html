About Me

Hello! Studying Geomatics Engineering at Hacettepe University, my focus centers around Geographic Information Systems (GIS), photogrammetry, remote sensing, and spatial data analytics. To put these concepts into practice, I work with tools like QGIS, Netcad, and MicroStation while integrating Python, JavaScript, and HTML/CSS for spatial data processing, interface design, and problem-solving. This space serves as an academic platform where I share my coursework and project implementations, connecting traditional geomatics principles with modern Web GIS technologies as I continue to learn and build.
